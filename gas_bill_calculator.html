How the calculation works

Gas usage is calculated from the difference between the two meter readings, converted into kilowatt-hours (kWh) using the conversion factors shown in your bill.

Imperial kWh = units × 2.83 × 1.02264 × 39 ÷ 3.6

Energy charge = kWh × unit rate. Standing charge = number of days × daily standing rate. VAT is added to the combined amount. The number of days is the difference between dates; for example, 23 October to 14 November is 22 days. Monetary line items are rounded to pennies.
