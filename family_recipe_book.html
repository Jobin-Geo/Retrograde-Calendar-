4. Cooking instructions
    Recipe notes & storage

    For children: Follow their appetite. Choose lower-salt cheese and sauces. Remove bones and whole spices, and cut food into suitable pieces. Avoid whole nuts for under-fives; blend cashews smoothly if already tolerated. No honey for babies under 12 months. Where a recipe includes chilli pastes or hot sauces, make the child's portion separately. Use mild spice blends.

    Storage: Refrigerate food within two hours and use within 48 hours or freeze. Cool rice within one hour and use refrigerated rice within 24 hours; freeze later portions. Defrost in the fridge and reheat once until steaming hot throughout. Cool children's portions before serving. Store salad, wraps and cold sauces separately.

    One meal per person per day. Original adult nutrition is shown before family adaptations. Ingredient ranges use their midpoints. Adjust water and seasoning for batch size.
