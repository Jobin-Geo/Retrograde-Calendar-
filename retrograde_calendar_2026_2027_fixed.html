What each planet represents

Traditional Jyotish planets and, optionally, modern Western planets.

☿ Mercury

Communication, learning, business, documents, travel and technology. Astrologers often suggest reviewing plans and double-checking details during Mercury retrograde.

♀ Venus

Relationships, art, beauty, comfort, luxury and personal tastes. Venus retrograde is sometimes treated as a period to reconsider expensive luxury purchases.

♂ Mars

Energy, initiative, courage, machinery and conflict. Traditional readings focus on reconsidering how action and effort are directed.

♃ Jupiter

Wisdom, learning, faith, teachers and long-term growth. Retrograde can symbolise reassessing priorities and beliefs.

♄ Saturn

Discipline, commitments, responsibility, persistence and consequences. Retrograde can symbolise reviewing duties and unfinished work.

♅ Uranus

Change, independence and innovation in modern Western astrology. Not one of the nine classical Jyotish grahas.

♆ Neptune

Dreams, imagination, ideals and uncertainty in modern Western astrology. Not a classical Jyotish graha.

♇ Pluto

Transformation, power and inner change in modern Western astrology. Not a classical Jyotish graha.

☊ Rahu

North lunar node. In traditional Jyotish, Rahu is associated with desire, ambition and unconventional experience; the mean node is always retrograde.

☋ Ketu

South lunar node. In traditional Jyotish, Ketu is associated with detachment, insight and release; the mean node is always retrograde.
